What we do

Chimney care from clean to inspect

From a thorough sweep to a Level 1 WETT SITE inspection, Enchanted keeps wood-burning systems safe, clean and code-compliant across Hamilton, Halton, Peel and Toronto.

Chimney Cleaning

Most cleanings are done from the inside with high-powered HEPA vacuums. Drop cloths protect floors while technicians scrub the flue and firebox and check for hazards.

WETT Inspections

Essential for insurance and real estate. Our Level 1 WETT SITE inspections verify that your wood-burning system is sound, clear and compliant with safety codes.

Chimney Services

Wildlife prevention screening, roof vent screening, B-vent painting and smoke guard installation to protect your home year-round.

Wood Burning Inserts

High-efficiency wood fireplace inserts, fully installed and serviced, to maximize heat and lower heating bills.

Gas Fireplaces & Logs

Sales, installation and repair of modern Kingsman gas fireplaces and premium RealFyre gas log sets.

Chimney Products

Lock-Top™ dampers, rain caps, animal screens, roof vent screening and smoke guards that keep systems working safely.

About Enchanted

Certified sweeps since 1989

Enchanted Chimney Sweeps Inc. are certified professional technicians who have serviced chimneys from Hamilton to Toronto since 1989. Owner Tim Hawley and the team provide informed, professional and reliable service, backed by a No-Mess Guarantee.

Technicians are certified by WETT (Wood Energy Technical Training) as sweeps, technicians and comprehensive inspectors, not just basic SITE inspectors. Enchanted belongs to the National Chimney Sweep Guild and the Technical Standards and Safety Authority (TSSA), and is fully insured.
